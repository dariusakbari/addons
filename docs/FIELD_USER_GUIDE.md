# GTE Odoo — Field User Quick Guide

Open odoo.greentechelectric.ca on your phone → Construction app.

## Start of day
1. Field & Safety → FLHAs → New. Pick project, describe today's task,
   tick ONLY the PPE you actually need, add every hazard WITH a control,
   have the crew sign on the Crew Sign-off tab. Finalize.
   The form will refuse to finalize if anything required is missing.
2. Toolbox talk day? Field & Safety → Toolbox Talks → New: topic, notes,
   attendee signatures → Finalize.

## During the day
- Question for the consultant? RFIs → New: subject, project, question,
  who it's addressed to → Open. The coordinator takes it from there.
- Something wrong on site? Incident Reports → New (what happened,
  severity, what you did immediately) → Submit. Serious? Call your
  supervisor too — the app notifies, it does not replace a phone call.
- Deficient work → NCRs → New. Deliveries/visitors/material leaving
  site → the matching form under Field & Safety.

## End of day
Daily Site Logs → New: crew hours on the Labour tab, what got done,
quantities, delays, photos, then sign on the Signature tab → Finalize.
Your PM gets it automatically.

## Rules of thumb
- If the form won't finalize, it is telling you what is missing — fill it.
- Never overwrite a drawing: upload the new file and use Register
  Revision in Drawings & Documents.
- You only see your own projects. Missing one? Ask your PM to add you.
